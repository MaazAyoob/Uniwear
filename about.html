OUR HERITAGE

Three Decades of
Textile Craftsmanship

Established in Bengaluru, Serving Worldwide

Founded in 1992, UNIWEAR emerged from a vision to revolutionize the workplace uniform ecosystem in India. Over the last 30+ years of dedicated textile craftsmanship, we have grown from a boutique tailoring enterprise in Bengaluru into a national workwear powerhouse, managing complex supply contracts and delivering premium garments for over 2,000+ corporate partners, heavy industrial conglomerates, healthcare systems, and hospitality brands globally.

Our core philosophy balances advanced textile innovation with strict structural ergonomics. Every garment we manufacture is built on a foundation of quality, ensuring maximum safety, comfort, and durability while functioning as a proud representation of our clients' core brand identity.
