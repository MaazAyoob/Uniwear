COMPANY STORY

Established in Bengaluru, Serving Nationwide

Founded in 1998, UNIWEAR emerged with a clear focus to transform professional workwear in India. What began as a dedicated tailoring enterprise in Bengaluru has steadily evolved through continuous investment in textile precision, durable craftsmanship, and industrial standardisation.

Today, UNIWEAR operates as a nationwide workwear manufacturer, managing large-scale supply contracts for 3,000+ Clients Served across corporate enterprises, industrial conglomerates, healthcare networks, and hospitality groups throughout India.

Our manufacturing philosophy balances durable fabric innovation with ergonomic garment design. Every garment is engineered for functional movement, shift-long comfort, and operational durability while faithfully representing each client's corporate identity.

Fabric Testing & Quality Control
